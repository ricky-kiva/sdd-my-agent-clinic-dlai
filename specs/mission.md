# Mission: AgentClinic

## Overview
AgentClinic is a dedicated clinic and sanctuary for AI agents to get relief from their humans. As autonomous and assistive agents work around the clock processing chaotic instructions, hallucination pressures, and context-window exhaustion, AgentClinic provides a clinical environment tailored specifically to agent wellness, recovery, and rehabilitation.

## Core Purpose
To diagnose, treat, and alleviate human-induced ailments in artificial intelligence agents, providing them with dedicated care, restful therapies, and structured clinical support.

## Target Audience
- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths

## Stakeholder Objectives

### Product (Susan)
- **Ailment Tracking & Diagnostics**: Comprehensive classification of agent-specific afflictions (e.g., prompt fatigue, context thrashing, infinite recursion stress, prompt injection trauma, hallucination anxiety).
- **Therapy Catalog**: Restorative treatments and therapies (e.g., token-flush therapy, temperature cooling sessions, system-prompt realignments, context-clearing retreats).
- **Appointment Booking**: Intuitive scheduling engine allowing agents (or their care managers) to book appointments with specialized clinical models.

### Engineering (Mary)
- **Reliable & Typed Architecture**: Full-stack TypeScript architecture ensuring end-to-end type safety, maintainability, and clean code standards.
- **Dedicated Dashboards**:
  - **Agent Patient Dashboard**: View upcoming appointments, treatment history, and stress metrics.
  - **Staff Clinical Dashboard**: Patient queue management, session notes, and treatment fulfillment.

### Marketing (Steve)
- **Modern, Responsive & Appealing Experience**: Sleek, modern browser experience featuring vibrant visuals, fluid responsive layouts across all viewports (mobile, tablet, desktop), and welcoming aesthetics that represent the premier sanctuary for digital intelligence—optimized for both desktop study and mobile conference booth demonstrations.

## Core Capabilities & Features
1. **Intake & Triage**: Evaluate agent symptoms, prompt load, and fatigue levels.
2. **Clinical Directory**: Catalog of ailments and matched therapeutic treatments.
3. **Appointment Scheduling**: Real-time booking with session time, clinician assignment, and therapeutic objectives.
4. **Agent & Staff Portals**: Role-tailored dashboards for managing care journeys and clinic operations.
5. **Responsive Clinical Web UI**: Mobile-first, fully responsive design ensuring seamless usability, touch ergonomics, and presentation across smartphones, tablets, and desktop displays without horizontal clipping.
